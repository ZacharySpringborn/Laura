Area Guide
What you need to know about your search communities

Red Feather Lakes sits at roughly 8,000 feet in the Roosevelt National Forest, about 60 miles northwest of Fort Collins. It’s a genuine mountain community — not a resort — with a general store, volunteer fire department, and neighbors who actually know each other. Crystal Lakes subdivision offers lake access. Elkridge is more private and rural, with larger lots and a quieter character.

Glacier View Meadows near Livermore offers larger lots (several listings run 1–3+ acres) at comparable prices, with a spread-out feel and impressive mountain views. Poudre Canyon along Highway 14 puts the Cache la Poudre River — Colorado’s only Wild & Scenic River — essentially at your doorstep. Bellvue properties sit lower in elevation and are more accessible year-round.

From Nebraska: I-76 or I-80 to I-25 North to Fort Collins, then Highway 287 or Rist Canyon Road north and west. Most of the search area is 90–105 minutes from Fort Collins. Road conditions in winter vary by community — worth factoring based on how often you’ll visit in snow season.

  • ✈
    World-Class Fly Fishing — Cache la Poudre River and multiple stocked lakes within your search area
  • 🏔
    Roosevelt National Forest Access — Hundreds of miles of hiking, biking, ATV, and snowmobile trails direct from many properties
  • ❄
    Four-Season Recreation — Cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, ice fishing in winter; hunting in fall
  • 👻
    Wildlife & Solitude — Elk, mule deer, black bear, and moose are common — genuine Rocky Mountain living
  • 📡
    Starlink Coverage — Satellite internet has transformed work-from-mountain viability; cell coverage varies by canyon
  • 🏠
    Second Home Community — Crystal Lakes and Glacier View Meadows are welcoming to part-time residents
  • ⛽
    Fort Collins Hub — Full services 45–60 min away: Costco, hospitals, restaurants, Denver International 90 min
  • 🌞
    Cool Summers — Highs typically in the 70s at 8,000 ft; winters require 4WD and snow tires on mountain roads
